# City art candidates — 18 October 2026

The free Quaternius Standard kit is now imported under `assets/city/quaternius_downtown`: 153 glTF models and 29 shared textures, with its CC0 license. The city uses its three complete building models, AC units, planters, manholes and asphalt/concrete textures. REVVED supplies placement, simple collisions, grouped rendering and terrain-following roads. Other candidates below have not been imported. Availability and pricing can change.

## Recommended modular kit

[Quaternius Downtown City MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/downtowncitymegakit.html): Boston/NYC-style textured buildings and street pieces; 315 models in the full kit, CC0. The free Standard tier includes approximately 60–70% of the kit in OBJ/FBX/glTF. The full Source tier with engine projects and extra shaders is paid. This is detailed stylized game art, not photorealistic architecture.

## More realistic alternatives

[Poly Haven Modular Urban Apartments Facade](https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_urban_apartments_facade): CC0 facade modules, glTF/FBX/Blender, including 1K/2K texture options. The full displayed set is about 118K triangles; use selected modules and simple collision. Requires assembling complete buildings, roofs and backs.

[Poly Haven Aerial Asphalt 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_asphalt_01): CC0 asphalt material, not road geometry. Suitable for our terrain-following roads after adding consistent metre-based UVs. Start with 1K/2K textures, normal and roughness maps, with separate road markings.

## Fab findings

[City Sample Buildings — Epic Games](https://www.fab.com/listings/008fe959-5511-428e-93bd-f99b1179f6d5): free, 2,000+ detailed modules, but explicitly UE-Only Content. Excluded from this Godot project.

[Buildings — Elbolillo](https://www.fab.com/listings/25f46dae-1467-4d2e-bba3-b9fb0f58efc8): free, ten buildings, FBX/DAE/GLB. Retro/low-poly style, so a weaker fit for the requested visual improvement. The retrieved public listing did not expose a concrete license selection; license remains unverified.

[DownTown Office — Replex](https://www.fab.com/listings/0c920ed7-64f9-4010-a099-5374b4f79bfb): 150+ detailed models, Unreal Engine format. Price was not exposed in the retrieved page; not confirmed free or directly Godot-ready.

[Fab Standard License summary](https://www.fab.com/eula) permits compatible tools beyond Unreal, but does not override a specific UE-Only restriction or make Unreal materials/Blueprints portable. Check the actual license and available source files before acquisition. CC0 candidates also suit the project's public repository more simply than restricted source-asset redistribution.

## Next art milestone

The first integration replaces ordinary building masses with imported architecture and street materials. Further art work can expand facade variety using the remaining individual modules, add street lighting/vegetation/signage and reduce repetition. The free kit does not include the paid Source project's window shaders. Landmark silhouettes remain approximate authored geometry.
